# Deck Brainstorm Brief: Sultai Medusa / Mythos

## Status
**Early concept, nothing locked.** This brief is for continuing the brainstorm with the user, not for building a list yet. Ask questions before proposing a commander.

## What the user wants
- Colors: black-blue-green (Sultai).
- Vibe: **Medusa / Greek mythos.** Gorgons, petrifying gaze, serpents, monsters.
- Same table standard as the user's other decks: casual, fun, not feel-bad. Light control is fine; don't optimize for it.
- No red (user's standing preference across brews).

## Sultai themes already mapped
**Mainstream**
- Graveyard value / reanimator (self-mill, recur, "nothing stays dead")
- +1/+1 counters and proliferate
- Ramp into big threats
- Mill as an engine feeding graveyard payoffs

**Niche / quirky**
- Sacrifice / aristocrats with recursion
- Devotion / permanents-matter
- Snakes and Nagas (Tarkir's Sultai clan is snake-coded)
- **Gorgons**: deathtouch and "destroy creatures this damages" effects (the petrify fantasy)
- **Hydras**: X-cost counter creatures, mythos-flavored

## Most promising direction
**Gorgon / Medusa "petrify" deck**, possibly blended with snakes/nagas and hydras for a full "mythic monsters" feel, on top of a graveyard-and-ramp foundation. Next step was to check how much real Gorgon support exists.

## Open questions to ask the user
1. Pure Gorgon/serpent tribal, or a broader "mythic monsters" mix (gorgons + snakes + hydras)?
2. Should the commander itself be a gorgon or naga, or is a non-tribal Sultai commander fine if it fits the flavor?
3. Budget: same ~$150 as the other decks?
4. How much graveyard play vs. creature combat?

## Research to do (verify, don't rely on memory)
- Scryfall: `t:gorgon id:bug legal:commander`, `(t:snake or t:naga) id:bug legal:commander`, `t:hydra id:bug legal:commander`
- Legendary candidates for the command zone in B/U/G (or B/G, U/G subsets) with gorgon, naga, or snake types, or deathtouch payoffs.
- Deathtouch enablers and "deathtouch matters" payoffs.

## Deliverable for the next session
Two or three commander options with how each plays at the table, then a locked brief in the same format as the other deck briefs.
